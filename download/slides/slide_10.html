Conclusion
Land Ledger proves that trustworthy digital land administration can be built end-to-end with open technology.
check_circle Every record hashed, every transfer fraud-screened before approval
check_circle Ownership settled on an immutable ledger with full provenance
check_circle Every action audited — available 24/7 from the cloud with nothing more than a browser and an internet connection.
