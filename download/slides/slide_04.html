PROPOSED SOLUTION

LAND LEDGER — a role-based web registry where every property, document and ownership transfer is cryptographically verifiable and settled on a blockchain ledger.

01
lightbulb Project idea

One integrated platform for citizens and registrars: register properties, upload deed documents, initiate transfers and trace provenance — with fraud screening built into the workflow itself.

02
account_tree Proposed approach

React SPA talks to an Express REST API; MongoDB Atlas stores users, properties, documents and transfers, while a Solidity smart contract (LandRegistry.sol) holds on-chain ownership truth. Documents are SHA-256 hashed at upload; every transfer is scored by a 9-check fraud engine before approval.

03
auto_awesome Key innovation

The smart contract itself rejects provenance mismatches — ownership can only move along the chain of legitimate owners. Fraud is screened at submission, not after the dispute, and every API action appends to an immutable audit stream streamed live to the dashboard.

04
flag Expected outcome

A single trusted source of property truth, buyer-verifiable provenance, fraud blocked before settlement, and 24/7 availability from MongoDB Atlas cloud — no local database to keep running.
