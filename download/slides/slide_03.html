RESEARCH / MARKET GAP

No existing platform unifies property registry, document integrity, transfer workflow, fraud screening and immutable audit in one always-on system. Land Ledger integrates all five layers and compares favorably on every axis — ownership truth moves on-chain, documents are SHA-256 sealed, and every action is auditable in real time.
