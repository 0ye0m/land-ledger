What problem are we addressing?

Land ownership still runs on fragmented paper registries and siloed digital portals. Title transfers depend on slow manual scrutiny, so records remain easy to forge and hard to verify. There is no single, tamper-proof source of truth for who owns what.

groups
Who is affected?

Citizens and buyers risk the same plot being sold to multiple parties. Registrars drown in manual verification workloads, while banks hesitate to lend against titles that cannot be cryptographically proven.

report_problem
Current challenges

Forged title deeds and duplicate sales of one property; no immutable ownership history visible to anyone; verification cycles that stretch for weeks; zero public audit trail; records scattered across departments and district offices.

public
Why is the problem important?

Land and property disputes drive roughly two-thirds of India civil litigation (Daksh access-to-justice study), locking property capital for decades. Secure, verifiable digital titling is a declared national priority under the DILRMP programme.
